The food and agri-food sector is one of the most active when it comes to trade shows. From innovation and sourcing to networking, these events are strategic gatherings for players across the supply chain. Here is our selection of the 10 shows you cannot afford to miss in 2026.

## 1. SIAL Paris

**Dates**: 17-21 October 2026 | **Venue**: Paris Nord Villepinte

SIAL remains the world's reference trade show for the food industry. With more than 7,000 exhibitors and 400,000 visitors from 200 countries, it is the unmissable event for discovering global trends and developing your international network.

## 2. Salon International de l'Agriculture (SIA)

**Dates**: 28 February - 8 March 2026 | **Venue**: Paris Expo Porte de Versailles

Beyond its public-facing programme, the SIA offers a first-rate professional space. It is the ideal place to meet upstream agricultural players and forge strategic partnerships.

## 3. CFIA Rennes

**Dates**: 10-12 March 2026 | **Venue**: Parc des Expositions de Rennes

The Carrefour des Fournisseurs de l'Industrie Agroalimentaire brings together more than 1,600 exhibitors specialising in ingredients, equipment, and packaging. A technical and practical show, ideal for sourcing.

## 4. Natexpo

**Dates**: 18-20 October 2026 | **Venue**: Lyon Eurexpo

The reference show for organic and eco-responsible products. With the continued growth of the organic market in France, Natexpo attracts more than 1,200 exhibitors and 15,000 professional visitors each year.

## 5. ALL4PACK Paris

**Dates**: November 2026 | **Venue**: Paris Nord Villepinte

Dedicated to packaging and logistics, ALL4PACK is essential for food industry manufacturers looking for innovative and sustainable packaging solutions.

## 6. Sirha Lyon

**Dates**: January 2027 (biennial) | **Venue**: Lyon Eurexpo

Although the next edition takes place in early 2027, Sirha deserves its place on this list. This world show for catering and hospitality welcomes more than 200,000 visitors and remains a major fixture for food service suppliers.

## 7. MDD Expo

**Dates**: March 2026 | **Venue**: Paris Expo Porte de Versailles

The show dedicated to own-label products. A key event for manufacturers looking to grow their private-label business with major retailers.

## 8. SANDWICH & SNACK SHOW

**Dates**: March 2026 | **Venue**: Paris Expo Porte de Versailles

A show that is growing rapidly, reflecting the rise of fast food and snacking. Ideal for innovations in food-to-go and on-the-move catering solutions.

## 9. IPA / SIMA

**Dates**: November 2026 | **Venue**: Paris Nord Villepinte

The trade show for technologies serving the food processing industry. Automation, robotics, digitalisation: the innovations on show at IPA are shaping the food factory of tomorrow.

## 10. Salon Vinitech-Sifel

**Dates**: December 2026 | **Venue**: Parc des Expositions de Bordeaux

For players in the wine and tree fruit sectors, Vinitech-Sifel is the reference technical event. More than 900 exhibitors present the latest innovations in viticulture, wine-making, and packaging.

## How to Choose the Right Shows?

Not all of these events will be relevant to every business. To make the right choice, consider:

- **Your positioning**: B2B industrial, food service, retail, organic?
- **Your objectives**: prospecting, market intelligence, sourcing, brand image?
- **Your budget**: an international show like SIAL requires a greater investment than a regional show
