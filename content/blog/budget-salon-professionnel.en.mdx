Exhibiting at a trade show is a powerful commercial lever, but costs can escalate quickly if you do not keep a close eye on each line of expenditure. Here is a comprehensive guide to building a realistic budget in 2026.

## The Essential Cost Categories

### Exhibition Space Rental

This is generally the largest budget item. Rates vary considerably depending on the show:

- **Regional trade shows**: 150 to 300 € / m²
- **National shows (Paris, Lyon)**: 300 to 600 € / m²
- **Major international shows**: 500 to 1,000 € / m²

For a first show, a stand of **9 to 12 m²** is a good balance between visibility and cost. Budget between **2,700 € and 7,200 €** for the space alone at a national show.

### Stand Design and Fit-Out

You have three main options:

- **Shell scheme** (you manage everything): 1,000 to 3,000 € for furniture and signage
- **Turnkey stand** offered by the organiser: 3,000 to 8,000 €
- **Custom-built stand** via a specialist contractor: 8,000 to 25,000 € and above

> The turnkey stand often offers the best value for money for SMEs exhibiting for the first time.

### Marketing and Communication Materials

Do not underestimate this category, which is frequently overlooked:

- **Brochures and flyers**: 300 to 800 €
- **Business cards**: 50 to 150 €
- **Pull-up banners / roller banners**: 150 to 400 € each
- **Branded gifts** (pens, tote bags, etc.): 500 to 2,000 €

### Staff Costs

Budget for travel and accommodation for your team:

- **Transport** (train, flight, vehicle): 100 to 500 € per person
- **Accommodation**: 100 to 200 € / night in the regions, 150 to 300 € / night in Paris
- **Meals**: 30 to 60 € / day per person

For a team of three people attending a three-day show in Paris, budget between **1,500 and 3,000 €** in ancillary expenses.

### Additional Costs Often Overlooked

- **Electricity and Wi-Fi** on the stand: 200 to 600 €
- **Insurance**: 100 to 300 €
- **Extra exhibitor badges**: 50 to 150 €
- **Parking**: 50 to 150 €

## Indicative Total Budget

| Company type | Stand | Estimated total budget |
|---|---|---|
| Micro-business / start-up | 9 m², basic stand | 5,000 to 10,000 € |
| SME | 12-18 m², turnkey stand | 10,000 to 25,000 € |
| Mid-cap / large group | 24 m²+, custom-built stand | 25,000 to 80,000 €+ |

## How to Optimise Your Budget

1. **Book early**: organisers often offer early-bird rates with 10 to 20% discounts
2. **Share costs**: split a stand with a complementary partner
3. **Reuse**: invest in modular furniture that can be used across multiple shows
4. **Negotiate**: end-of-aisle spots or locations in less-trafficked areas cost less
